Dream World Magazine

This magazine was created as part of a course during my studies. The layout of the brochure is based on a clear design concept that is composed of various typographic and design principles.
The type area is based on the Fibonacci sequence (..., 8, 13, 21, ...) and is divided into five columns. The height of the layout is structured by the golden ratio (1:1.618) - a principle that is also reflected in the chosen font sizes.
I combined two fonts for the typographic design: Cooper Black Regular creates a striking visual effect with its playful yet legible character. Unna was used for continuous text.

Grauzone

zwischen Kunst und Vandalismus

This short documentary deals with the question of how graffiti influences our society - between artistic expression and the legal gray area.
The project was created in 2025 as part of my studies in collaboration with Maya Terler. The focus was on combining animated statistics and real film footage.
My tasks included storytelling, camera work and the design and implementation of the animations. I was also responsible for the poster design.

BMW Art-Car Magazine #4

This free project was created in the summer of 2024. The impetus came from a friend who asked me to paint his drift car.
The magazine takes up this idea in its design: Three continuous stripes run dynamically through all pages. The typography is deliberately kept simple.
The visual language is characterized by a halftone effect. This issue is number 4 because it is dedicated exclusively to the fourth BMW Artcar designed by Andy Warhol.

Gemeinsam Selbstständig

mit Ergotherapie zurück in den Alltag

This five-minute short documentary gives an insight into the world of occupational therapy and shows how it helps people to organize their everyday lives independently.
The project was created as part of a media design course. My tasks included camera work, editing, animating the belly bands and organizing the interviews.
The aim was to convey as positive and realistic a picture of occupational therapy as possible.

Humppy

The Humppy app is a happiness challenge app that motivates users to complete small social or ecological tasks every day. By completing the tasks, you collect XP and rise in level.
As part of the screen design course, I developed and designed the app as a concept - from the idea to the user interface to the first prototypes.
