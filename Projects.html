Bling-Data

This project was created as part of my Bachelor's thesis in Information Design at FH JOANNEUM. It's a magazine that uses infographics to visually explore the different meanings behind the status symbols shown in trap music videos by female and male artists.

Matchday

These graphics were created together with DailyDrama Studio, including story posts for World Cup, DFB Cup, and Bundesliga matches for various players.

Dream World Magazine

The magazine's design is strongly inspired by Tyler, The Creator's album "Call Me If You Get Lost". It features four posters I designed, each broken down and discussed in terms of the design principles applied.

BMW Art-Car Magazine #4

This independent project was designed by me. The inspiration came from a friend whose BMW I helped paint.

Branding

3D-Animations

Dokumentationen

Gemeinsam Selbstständig & Grauzone

I've contributed to two documentaries so far, from filming all the way through to the final edit.
"Gemeinsam Selbstständig" is a production that introduces viewers to the world of occupational therapy and explains how it works.
"Grauzone" is a project about graffiti and street art, and how society perceives this form of art.
